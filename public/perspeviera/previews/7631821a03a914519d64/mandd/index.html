mandd’s Expanded Survey

First, what do you want? Pick your intention, then work out what to build, what you can put in, and what should come out of it.

Based on Blackknives’ idea, expanded by mandd into specific questions about intentions, commitments and results.

Start with your intention · Choose options, then add details0 of 10 answered
What do you want?

Start with what you are here to do. Pick the closest fit.

State your intention.

Are you looking to make a profit, build without a profit goal, do both, or are you still deciding?

What do you want to build?

Name the thing, the problem it solves, and who it is for. A rough idea is enough.

How do you want to build it?

Choose a starting approach, then add the first step, tools or people you need.

What do you want from it?

Pick as many as fit. People can want different things from the same project.

What are you willing to put in?

Select what you can offer. Add concrete limits so others know what to expect.

How much time can you actually give?

Choose a realistic starting amount. It is fine if your availability varies.

When do you expect something to happen?

Pick a starting window, then say when you would like the first useful result.

What results are you expecting?

Describe what should improve or become possible, and how you would know whether it helped.

What would a completed project look like?

Describe exactly what exists at the end. What must someone be able to use, see or test before you call it finished?
