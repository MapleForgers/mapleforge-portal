A proposed survey · For review

Survey based on Blackknives’ proposal

Find out why people are here, what would help them, and which groups have people ready to participate.

Suggested questions based on Blackknives’ recommendations, prepared for her review. This is not a questionnaire she has written or approved.

9 questions · Short, practical answers are fine0 of 9 answered
01 What should we call you?

A nickname is enough. Add a little about yourself if you want.

02 What are you looking for here?

Choose everything that fits. These categories follow the suggestions in Blackknives’ proposal.

03 Which of those matters most to you right now?

Choose one starting priority. This helps work out what to support first.

04 What would be useful to you first?

A concrete request helps people connect you to the right place. No need to share personal or sensitive details.

05 What could you help other members with?

Skills, practical experience, resources or introductions. Sales experience, HR, support work and technical skills can all help. It is fine to be here mainly to learn or find support.

06 How would you like to take part?

Select what suits you now. This is an expression of interest, not a work commitment.

07 What kind of group would help you?

An existing channel, a small working group, a support space, or something missing from the categories above. Describe what people would actually do there.

08 Do you have a project to start or join?

If so, describe it and the people or roles you need. Would it be open to everyone or looking for particular skills? Skip this if projects are not why you joined.

09 What would help your group work well?

For example: a channel, a simple posting template, introductions, shared tools, or help organizing. What should the group decide for itself?
