A proposal by mandd

Constitution board

Can a bunch of strangers agree on how to govern themselves, then build something together?

How I see it happening

  1. Stage 1

    Form a group

    Find people willing to work this out together. We can choose a project later.

  2. Stage 2

    Defragment the ideas

    Post rules and agreements. Comment, revise, upvote and downvote. Let the board settle.

  3. Stage 3

    Build a working template

    Look at the results. Can we turn the supported ideas into rules, roles and a way to decide?

“I've said my piece, cast my votes, and have nothing else to add for now.”

When everyone reaches that point, we look at what rose to the top, what fell, and where we still disagree.

What I hope comes out of it

A first virtual constitution for this pod to agree on and test. Votes help us see where we stand; we still need to resolve contradictions and see whether the pieces work together.
